5312 Samels Rd: Offer & Closing Terms Strategy

Buyer-side approach for Clark & Jenny. Price targets are kept private.

← Back to project

Our position

  • All-cash purchase, so there's no appraisal or financing risk.
  • Flexible closing date, chosen by the seller.
  • 81+ days on market, two price cuts, and a prior contract that fell through.
  • Market is buyer-leaning heading into winter.

Price justification

  • 2nd barn has no building permit, and the pole barn permit was terminated in 2012. We'll ask for after-the-fact permits before closing, or a credit.
  • Dated baths and finishes; modern remodel planned.
  • Unknown well/septic records; 29-year-old systems.
  • Small above-grade footprint (1,480 sf).

Contingencies

  • Home inspection
  • Well water quality and flow test
  • Septic inspection
  • Permit and records review
  • Title, survey, and private road agreement
  • Disclosures: panel amperage, utility provider

Possession at closing

No Michigan law requires a month of seller occupancy after closing. It's a negotiable contract term (sample Michigan sales contract ¶26–27).

  • Close when the seller is ready, and hand over keys at closing.
  • Final walk-through within 48 hours before closing.
  • House, both barns, and the grounds vacant and broom-clean, with all personal property and debris removed.
  • Itemized inclusions and exclusions (boat, workbench, and others).
  • Escrow holdback at the title company until the walk-through passes, with removal costs paid from it.

If the seller needs time

  • Short written occupancy license (days, not a month)
  • Daily occupancy charge plus a holdover penalty
  • Sizable occupancy and damage escrow
  • Seller pays utilities and insurance, is liable for damage, and makes no alterations

Reference: post-closing occupancy checklist.

Give vs. hold

Give: close date, fast inspection window, no appraisal.

Hold: vacant possession and escrow, permit remedy, well/septic contingencies.

Not legal advice. Contract language to be reviewed with our agent or a Michigan attorney.
